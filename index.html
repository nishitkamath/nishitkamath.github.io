04 · Design work

Things he has designed

From products for brands in Bangkok to drones in California and streets in Pune.

Bangkok freelance · 2007–10

Products for brands

Tennis-camp programmes and flyers for the Bangkok Tennis Academy (from 2007); promo products for hotel, spa, golf and football brands; wine and home products; headphones; a camera case.

Godrej Interio · 2010–11

Bathroom storage concept

What would have been India's first truly indigenous, customisable, fit-ready bathroom storage line; plus office systems and furniture concepts.

The CarLab · 2015–16

Car clinics for global brands

Automotive clinics and focus groups for Acura, Subaru, Honda, Toyota, Mahindra, Karma and Nissan; a study on the future of the automobile.

Rivian · 2016

Customer experience strategy + mobility operations design

Clinics and field research for Rivian's first vehicles, later the R1T and R1S, plus research to help differently abled people learn electric cars faster.

Flyspan Systems · 2015–20

Drone systems

As chief design officer: four drone systems and an emergency-delivery app. It grew from Air Hound, his ArtCenter thesis: rescue drones carrying NASA JPL’s FINDER heartbeat radar.

VNV Printlab · 2019–

Design prototyping that sells

Scale models for the Uber–Delhi Metro launch; products for Unilever, The Times Group and Godrej Consumer Products.

VNV Printlab · 2019–

Custom moulds and instruments

For chocolates, cakes, desserts and ice creams, for names such as Burma Burma, Bon Fiction, Masque and The Bombay Canteen.

VNV Educate · 2020–21

A design academy

Short courses in 3D sketching, design thinking, CAD and 3D printing for children, adults and professionals.

DeAuto hackathon · 2023

Self-driving classroom

BATU: a mobile classroom for children on a self-driving vehicle, built in a 31-day global design hackathon: 2nd place.

WBG India · 2024–

EV super charging

DC charging for passenger cars, heavy vehicles and marine use, in India and Southeast Asia.

TSKN · PPCR · PARC · 2023–

Re-designing Pune's streets

Starting in his own neighbourhood: a traffic-flow plan, walkability, zebra crossings, box junctions, a hop-on hop-off shuttle. Then the city: the Pune Traffic Mitigation Initiative, which became a Traffic Police internship.

WorldSkills · 2019–2026

Competition tooling

Custom bakery and confectionery tooling for WorldSkills competitors: 2019, 2022, 2024 and Shanghai 2026.
